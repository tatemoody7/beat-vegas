## The decision

What should someone who is not you be able to get from Beat Vegas this season: the public site
alone, the site plus a free weekly email, or that plus a path to a paid tier in 2027?

**My recommendation is Option B:** keep the site as the product and add a free Sunday email that
also carries each BET-rated pick before kickoff. The email is the cheapest proof a self-hosted
record can have: a pick sent to readers before the game cannot be edited afterwards. The paid
question waits for a dated 2027 gate (Option C's rules) rather than being decided now.

## Where the record stands (the honest constraint on every option)

- Real money is **10-10, −0.29u** through week 5 (Wilson 29.9–70.1%). That is inside its
  interval either way: there is no demonstrated edge yet, and H-STOP-2 has read one week.
- The model is less accurate than Hard Rock (week 5: MAE 9.03 vs 8.39). The site already says the
  edge "if any, is small and unconfirmed".
- So "follow our picks" cannot be the pitch this season. What exists, and what nobody in the
  CFB data world sells, is the **judged output**: a pre-registered rule, every real ticket, its
  close and its interval, with losses in the first line (`CFBD_BUSINESS_TEARDOWN.md` §7).

### Option C's gate, if you want it written now
A paid rung is considered in 2027 only if all of these hold, checked on a fixed date:
1. H-STOP-2 has not hit its failure boundary, and real money has a full season on the ledger.
2. A written yes from the payment platform (Whop, or Stripe through the newsletter host).
3. A Florida gaming lawyer's read on § 849.14 and FDUTPA for a paid tipster
   (`LEGAL_MEMO_2026-10.md` §8, items 1–6).
4. What is sold is never earlier or better picks than the free list gets (`CFBD_BUSINESS_TEARDOWN.md`
   §7, "practices to avoid"); the candidates on file are infrastructure: the ledger export and the
   research write-ups.

## Rules every option keeps (already true, or one line of copy)

- The whole record since the first ticket, losses included, in units with its interval; never a
  window, a streak or a "lock"; never "guaranteed", "inside information" or "low-risk"
  (FTC 16 CFR 255; the Zeidman case).
- Every public surface and email: "Not betting advice. For readers 21+ in Florida betting on Hard
  Rock Bet. Past results do not predict future results. Gambling problem? 1-833-PLAYWISE."
- "Odds via The Odds API" and "Data provided by CollegeFootballData.com"; lines shown per pick,
  never a standalone lines sheet or data download.
- No bet-slip screenshots or Hard Rock marks, no referral links, no betting for anyone else.

## Not recommended

- **A Discord or community channel.** It is Bill's support cost and retention engine at once
  (`KNOWLEDGE.md`: one announcements channel "because otherwise I get 20 people all messaging");
  the board is our failure surface and you have no support hours to give it.
- **Selling inputs** (data packs, an API): our vendors' terms forbid it and it is Bill's business,
  not ours.
- **A third-party "verified" badge** (Pikkit, Action Network BetSync): each needs a login to your
  Hard Rock account, which its terms forbid; the email timestamp does the same job.
