## 0. Canonical vocabulary (enforce globally)

| Concept | One term | Kill these |
|---|---|---|
| 0–100 colored number | **score** | "edge score", "rating", "under score" |
| line − our number | **gap** | "edge" (Research), "bv_gap", "HR gap", "Gap σ" |
| model's first-half estimate | **our number** | "Our #", "ours", "BV line", "the model's number", "Our reference 1H" |
| Hard Rock's posted 1H total | **Hard Rock's line** | "HR line", "hr" |
| consensus of other books | **the market line (DraftKings, FanDuel)** | "consensus 1H", "the market's number" |
| number derived from the full-game total | **our reference line (from the full-game total)** | "derived line", "estimated line", "proxy line", "derived_fg" |
| did the line move your way after you bet | **line value** | "CLV", "Avg CLV", "Avg line value", "closing-line value", "Price CLV" |
| amber tier | **Watch** | "EDGE" |
| the number/price at which it stops being a bet | **kill line / kill price** | "kill point", "kill number" |
| pick with no money on it | **paper pick** | "notional", "paper logged" |
| 5-bets-a-week ceiling | **weekly cap** | "cap-5", "over cap" |

`50 = breakeven` is now the only anchoring. Delete every "coin flip" used as a synonym for 50. Keep "close to a coin flip" **only** in the trust page, where it describes one game's outcome noise, not a probability of 50.

## 20. `/trust` — "How much to trust this" (full page, 161 words)

Heading: **How much to trust this**

> Beat Vegas rates one market: the first-half under.
>
> Our number is a model estimate of first-half points. It never sees the sportsbook line, so comparing the two is a real comparison. The gap between them is how we rank games.
>
> We cannot prove the gap makes money. Free historical data has no first-half lines, so past seasons were graded against an estimated line. Against that estimate, the biggest gaps did not clear the break-even rate you need at -110 ({`BREAKEVEN_PCT`}%). The backtest supports the ordering of games, not a profit.
>
> One first half is close to a coin flip. Our number misses a typical game by about {`SIGMA_PTS`} points, far more than any gap. A score only means something across hundreds of bets.
>
> So the real test is the live record: Hard Rock's actual lines, the bets you log, and line value. It is on **Results**. Until that record is large, read every score as an opinion with a number on it.

**Delete these caveats everywhere else** (they are now said once): `page.tsx:183`, `GameCard.tsx:266`, `verdict.ts:140–147`, `research/page.tsx:100,104,186,338`, `PostMortemPanel.tsx:229,500`, `glossary` "Bettable band" / "Margin of error" / "Proxy-graded backtest".
